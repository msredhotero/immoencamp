Our story

In Encamp since 1982

Immobiliària d'Encamp was founded in 1982 in Encamp, in the centre of Andorra. We have worked in real estate from the same village for more than forty years.

We sell new and resale homes, chalets and estates; we manage long-term and seasonal lets, business transfers, offices and commercial premises, and we help with financing.

We advise clients from Andorra and abroad who want to invest in the country or settle there, and we support them throughout the process.
